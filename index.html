Help centre

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Email hello@example.com and our team will reply within one business day.

Are there booking or service fees?

No. The price on each listing is the total you pay at checkout, including taxes and booking costs.

Can I get a refund if an event is cancelled?

Yes. If an organiser cancels or reschedules, you can request a full refund to your original payment method within 30 days.

Do I need an account to buy tickets?

No. You can browse and check out as a guest. An account is optional and lets you save favourite venues and view past orders.

How will I receive my tickets?

Mobile tickets with a QR code are emailed to you as soon as your payment is confirmed. Show them on your phone at the entrance.

Is there wheelchair-accessible seating?

Most partner venues offer accessible seating and companion tickets. Look for the accessibility details on each event page, or contact us before booking.
