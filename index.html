The fine print (it's short)

Does it cost anything?
No. There are no payments, in-game purchases, bets or prizes. It's free.
Do I need an account?
No. Nothing to sign up for and nothing to download.
Can I play against people online?
Not here. There's no chat or online multiplayer. Knockout is single-player, and the invite link is just a link you share with friends you already know.
What do you keep about me?
Nothing. Your best score only lives in this tab and disappears when you close it. The game-night name you type goes into the link and nowhere else.
