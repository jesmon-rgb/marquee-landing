Booking in three steps

Everything happens right here on the website — no app download required.

Pick a show

Browse listings with full details: date, venue, running time, age rating and total price.

Choose your seats

See the seating map, compare sections and hold your seats for 10 minutes while you check out.

Get mobile tickets

Tickets arrive by email instantly. Show the QR code at the door — printing is optional.

Questions, answered

Are there booking or service fees?

No hidden fees. The price on the listing is the total you pay at checkout, including taxes.

Can I get a refund if an event is cancelled?

Yes. If an organiser cancels or reschedules, you can request a full refund to your original payment method within 30 days.

Do I need an account to buy tickets?

No. You can browse and check out as a guest. An account is optional and lets you save favourite venues and see past orders.

How do I get my tickets?

Mobile tickets with a QR code are emailed to you immediately after purchase. Just show them on your phone at the entrance.
