A few things to know.

What can I do here?

Neon Knockout is a single-player reflex game. Table Talk draws conversation prompts for friends. Slow Sunday is a simple breathing timer. Wildside makes an adventure-night invite; it is not a playable adventure game.

Does it cost anything?

These activities are free. There are no payments, bets, cash prizes, or in-game purchases.

Can I chat or play online with other people?

There is no live chat, matchmaking, or online multiplayer here. Share an invite with people you know and use your usual chat or game to meet up.

Privacy & saved data

Your nickname and most recent game-night name are stored in this browser using local storage. This page has no account system, payment forms, or analytics scripts. Invite links include the game-night name, so leave personal information out of it. Photos load from Unsplash, which receives normal image requests from your browser. Your hosting provider may also keep standard request logs.
