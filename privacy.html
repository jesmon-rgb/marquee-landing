Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: we don't collect personal data. Here is the long version, which is still quite short.

Last updated 3 October 2026.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Playroom has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no cookies, no analytics or tracking scripts, and no advertising trackers.

Your scores

Drill results are kept in your browser's memory while the page is open. They are never sent to us and disappear when you close or reload the tab.

Squad links

When you make a squad link, the session name you type is placed in the link itself. It is not sent to or stored by us. Anyone you share the link with can read that name, so leave out personal information such as phone numbers or addresses.

Services that load with the page

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) to run and secure the service.
  • Fonts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives a normal web request from your browser, including your IP address.

Children

Playroom is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
